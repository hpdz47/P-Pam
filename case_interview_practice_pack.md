# Case Interview Practice Pack

MBB & Management Consulting Academy by Pam

---

## How to Use This Practice Pack

**For each question:**
1. Spend 1–2 minutes structuring the problem first
2. State and justify your assumptions clearly
3. Solve calculations step-by-step without a calculator
4. Narrate your thinking out loud throughout the process
5. Sanity-check whether your final answer feels directionally reasonable

**How to Get the Most Value from This Pack**
- Focus on structure before speed
- Prioritise logic over perfect accuracy
- Use easy round numbers whenever possible
- Practise communicating clearly under pressure
- Reattempt questions using different assumptions and frameworks

## Section 1: Population Based

*Difficulty level: Population Based*

### 1. How many iPhones are sold in Thailand each year?

**Recommended Structure**
Thailand population → smartphone users → iPhone penetration → replacement cycle → annual sales

**Guidance**
Consider:
- Different age groups
- Smartphone penetration by generation
- iPhone affordability
- Replacement behaviour

**Step 1 — Determine Thailand Population**
Assume:
- Thailand population ≈ 70M
- Even age distribution

**Step 2 — Segment by Age Group**

| Generation | Population | Smartphone Users | iPhone Users |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–20 | 20M | 12M | 5M |
| 21–40 | 20M | 19M | 10M |
| 41–60 | 20M | 16M | 5M |
| 61–80 | 10M | 5M | 1M |
| **Total** | **70M** | **52M** | **21M** |

**Step 3 — Estimate Replacement Cycle**
Assume:
- Average replacement cycle ≈ 3 years
- ~80% upgrade rate

21M ÷ 3 × 80% ≈ 6M replacement purchases

**Step 4 — Estimate New Switchers**
Assume:
- ~1–2M Android users switch annually

**Final Estimate ≈ 7–8M iPhones sold annually**

## Section 2: Household Based

*Difficulty level: Household Based*

### 1. How many households in Thailand subscribe to Netflix?

**Recommended Structure**
Thailand population → households → internet-connected households → target streaming households → Netflix penetration

**Step 1 — Determine Thailand Population**
Assume: Thailand population ≈ 70M

**Step 2 — Convert Population into Households**
Household-based questions should generally begin by converting population into households.
Assume: Average household size ≈ 3 people per household

70M ÷ 3 ≈ 23M households

**Step 3 — Estimate Internet-Connected Households**
Netflix requires:
- internet access
- connected devices
- digital payment capability

Assume:
- Internet penetration ≈ 85%

23M × 85% ≈ 20M internet-connected households

**Step 4 — Eliminate Non-Target Households**
Not all connected households are realistic Netflix customers. For example:
- some households may prefer free TV
- some may not stream regularly
- some may not be willing to pay for subscriptions

Assume:
- ~50% are realistic streaming households

20M × 50% ≈ 10M target households

**Step 5 — Estimate Netflix Penetration**
Netflix competes with:
- YouTube
- Disney+
- local streaming platforms
- piracy / free alternatives

Assume:
- Netflix penetration ≈ 40% among target households

10M × 40% ≈ 4M Netflix households

## Section 3: Worldwide Market Sizing

*Difficulty level: Worldwide*

### 1. What is the worldwide market size for bulletproof auto glass?

**Step 1 — Estimate One Representative Market First**
Rather than estimating every country individually, estimate Thailand first and extrapolate globally.

**Step 2 — Estimate Thailand Households**
Assume:
- Thailand population ≈ 70M
- Household size ≈ 3 people

70M ÷ 3 ≈ 23M households

**Step 3 — Segment by Income**

| Income Group | Households | Avg Cars per Household |
|---|---|---|
| High Income | 2M | 2 cars |
| Middle Income | 11M | 1 car |
| Low Income | 10M | 0.3 cars |

≈ 18M total vehicles

**Step 4 — Estimate BPAG Penetration**
Bulletproof auto glass is concentrated among:
- government vehicles
- embassies
- security firms
- luxury vehicles
- armoured trucks

Assume:
- ~0.1% of vehicles require BPAG

18M × 0.1% ≈ 18K vehicles
Round down conservatively: ≈ 10K BPAG vehicles in Thailand

**Step 5 — Extrapolate Globally**
Assume:
- Thailand represents ≈ 1% of global BPAG demand

10K ÷ 1% ≈ 1M vehicles globally

**Final Estimate ≈ 1M BPAG vehicles globally**

### 2. How many electric vehicles are sold globally each year?

**Step 1 — Decide on the Estimation Approach**
Rather than estimating one country and extrapolating, a cleaner approach is to estimate:
Global households → vehicle-owning households → vehicle replacement cycle → EV penetration

This creates a more direct and defendable structure.

**Step 2 — Estimate Global Population**
Assume: Global population ≈ 8B people

**Step 3 — Convert Population into Households**
Assume: Average household size globally ≈ 4 people

8B ÷ 4 ≈ 2B households

**Step 4 — Estimate Vehicle-Owning Households**
Vehicle ownership varies significantly:
- High in developed markets
- Lower in emerging economies

Assume:
- ~50% of households own at least one vehicle

2B × 50% ≈ 1B vehicle-owning households

**Step 5 — Estimate Annual Vehicle Replacement Demand**
Vehicles are not replaced annually.
Assume: Average replacement cycle ≈ 10 years

1B vehicles ÷ 10 years ≈ 100M vehicle sales annually

**Step 6 — Estimate EV Penetration**
EV adoption is driven by:
- government incentives
- charging infrastructure
- environmental regulations
- Chinese EV manufacturers
- declining battery costs

Assume: EV penetration ≈ 20%

100M × 20% ≈ 20M EV sales annually

**Final Estimate ≈ 15–20M electric vehicles sold globally each year**
